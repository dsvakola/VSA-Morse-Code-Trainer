# Privacy Policy — VSA Morse Code Trainer

**Effective date:** 17 November 2026
**Developed by:** Prof. Dattaraj S. Vidyasagar & Prof. Yash D. Vidyasagar, for Vidyasagar Academy
**Website:** [https://vsa.edu.in/software/](https://vsa.edu.in/software/) · **Contact:** [softinquiry@vsa.edu.in](mailto:softinquiry@vsa.edu.in)

## In short

The app works fully offline, collects no personal data, sends nothing over the internet, and keeps all progress only on your computer.

## What the app stores

The app saves your settings (speed, tone, volume and similar), your learning progress, quiz results and, if a teacher uses it, the class roster you type in. These are saved as files in the app's own data folder on your computer (`%APPDATA%\in.edu.vsa.morse-code-trainer`). They never leave your computer unless you export them yourself.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, sell or share personal information.
- It does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking of any kind.
- It does not need an account or a sign-in.
- It does not connect to the internet. Links in the About and Ham Community pages (the VSA software page, the VSA e-mail address and a fixed list of amateur radio clubs, practice sites and forums) open only when you click them, in your own browser or mail program. What those sites do is governed by their own privacy policies.

## Files you choose to open or save

When you export a WAV sound file, a PDF chart, a backup or a class report, the file is written only to the place you choose. When you open an audio recording to decode it, the recording is read on your computer and is not sent anywhere.

## Children

The app is suitable for students of all ages. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the app removes the program. To remove your saved progress as well, delete the folder `%APPDATA%\in.edu.vsa.morse-code-trainer`.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the app and on [https://vsa.edu.in/software/](https://vsa.edu.in/software/), with a new effective date.
